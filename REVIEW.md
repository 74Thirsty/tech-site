# REVIEW STANDARDS

The review must:

- Be honest
- Avoid advertising language
- Separate facts from opinions
- Identify uncertainty
- Mention limitations
- Consider different types of buyers
- Explain tradeoffs

Never say:
"best product ever"

Instead explain:
"best for this specific person because..."

---

# RESEARCH REQUIREMENTS

When possible analyze:

- warranty information
- update history
